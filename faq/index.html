ABOUT SALIMGPT

About SalimGPT

01 What is SalimGPT?

SalimGPT is an independent, research-based English documentary media initiative. History, science, society, education and lifestyle, technology, mystery, investigation, human civilization, and historical-social topics are presented through research and documentary storytelling.

02 Who is the founder and director of SalimGPT?

SalimGPT's founder and director is Mohammad Salim. Topic selection, research direction, fact review, scripting, editorial framing, visual planning, editing, and the overall direction of final publication are managed under his direction.

Learn more about the Director
03 What is SalimGPT's main goal?

SalimGPT's main goal is to transform complex, important, or curiosity-driven topics into clear, engaging, context-aware documentary stories grounded in research.

Maintaining knowledge value alongside engaging storytelling is part of the production philosophy.

04 Is SalimGPT an official OpenAI or ChatGPT project?

No. SalimGPT is an independent documentary media brand. The “GPT” portion of the brand name does not indicate official ownership, partnership, sponsorship, or endorsement by OpenAI, ChatGPT, or any other AI provider.

05 Where is SalimGPT operated from?

SalimGPT is an independent digital documentary initiative based in Bangladesh. Production direction is managed from Naikhongchhari, Bandarban, Bangladesh.

RESEARCH & FACT-CHECKING

Research, Sources & Fact-Checking

06 How is documentary research conducted?

Depending on the topic, research may include primary documents, official data, peer-reviewed research, institutional reports, credible books, reputable archives, established journalism, and other relevant web sources.

Not every source is given equal evidentiary weight.

07 How does SalimGPT cross-check important information?

Important factual claims are cross-checked with multiple credible sources whenever possible. Publication date, original source, methodology, geographic context, terminology, and conflicting evidence may all be considered.

View the Fact-Checking & Sources Policy
08 Does SalimGPT claim that all information is “100% verified”?

No. Historical evidence may be incomplete, scientific knowledge may evolve, statistics may be updated, and credible sources may disagree.

SalimGPT therefore does not claim absolute certainty. The goal is to produce the most accurate, transparent, and defensible presentation possible based on available evidence.

09 What happens when two credible sources conflict?

The sources may be compared by date, methodology, sample, definitions, institutional credibility, primary records, and underlying evidence.

If the conflict cannot be resolved, the documentary may represent the uncertainty or disagreement explicitly.

10 Is a primary source always the most accurate?

No. Primary sources are important, but they may contain bias, errors, incomplete information, or self-interest.

A primary source may therefore need to be considered alongside context, corroborating evidence, and relevant secondary analysis.

11 Does SalimGPT take extra care with scientific or medical claims?

Yes. A single study should not automatically be presented as scientific consensus. Evidence level, study design, sample, and limitations may need to be considered.

Health-related or drug-related documentary topics are approached with responsible wording rather than sensational certainty.

12 What happens if a factual error is discovered later?

If credible evidence identifies a material factual error, the written article, description, note, related text, or future edition may be updated where the format allows.

Knowingly reinforcing a known error is not part of SalimGPT's editorial policy.

03
AI & NARRATION

AI, Narration & Human Control

13 Does SalimGPT use AI?

Yes. AI may be used for research organization, language assistance, query generation, note comparison, narration, visual generation, and some parts of production.

However, AI output is not treated as final truth or an automatic editorial decision.

Read the AI Policy
14 Is SalimGPT documentary narration AI-generated?

Documentary narration may be synthetic or AI-generated. Voice generation is a production tool.

The wording, factual content, structure, and final editorial meaning remain human-controlled.

15 Is AI the final source for SalimGPT research?

No. AI may assist with discovery, summarization, translation, organization, or research queries, but material claims should be checked against real sources.

AI can hallucinate, so an AI-generated statement is not sufficient as a source by itself.

16 Who makes the final editorial decisions?

Final editorial judgment, source acceptance, narrative framing, visual selection, and publication decisions are human-led.

SalimGPT's overall creative and editorial direction is managed under Mohammad Salim.

VISUAL & MEDIA

Images, Footage & Visual Sources

17 Are all SalimGPT visuals real footage?

No. A documentary may include real footage, archival material, licensed images, permission-based media, public-domain material, maps, graphics, AI-generated images, and AI-assisted footage.

18 Are AI-generated historical scenes presented as real archives?

SalimGPT's policy is not to present illustrative or reconstructed AI-generated scenes misleadingly as authentic historical camera footage or fabricated archival evidence.

The purpose of AI visuals is explanation and visualization, not manufacturing evidence.

19 Are images found through Google Images used directly?

Google Search or Google Images may be a starting point for visual discovery and reference, but a search result is not itself permission to use media.

When direct use is needed, the original source, creator, license, attribution, and permission may be considered separately.

20 Can SalimGPT pay for media licenses?

Yes. If an archive, photographer, agency, stock library, or rights holder requires a license fee for commercial use, paid permission may be obtained when necessary.

Payment does not automatically mean that the original copyright ownership transfers to SalimGPT.

21 Does SalimGPT reupload completed videos from other creators?

SalimGPT's production model is not based on directly reuploading completed videos from other creators.

When third-party material is needed, it may be integrated into a broader research, narration, and editorial structure according to applicable permission, license, or lawful-use conditions.

COPYRIGHT & CONTENT USE

Copyright, Sharing & Reuse

22 Can I share a SalimGPT documentary link?

Yes. Responsible sharing of original website, YouTube, or official social links is generally welcome.

Platform-provided sharing or official embed features may also be used according to platform rules.

23 Can I quote from a SalimGPT article?

Limited quotations may be used with attribution for review, criticism, research, reporting, or educational reference.

A complete article, full script, or substantial portion should not be copied in a way that substitutes for the original publication.

24 Can I download a SalimGPT documentary and upload it to my own channel?

Full reuploading is generally not permitted. Permission may be required before downloading and republishing content on YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, a website, an app, or another platform.

25 How should I credit SalimGPT when using its content?

When possible, include the SalimGPT name, the relevant documentary or article title, and the original source link.

Writing only “Source: Internet” or “Source: Google” is not sufficient attribution.

26 Are SalimGPT AI-generated visuals freely reusable?

Public visibility does not automatically create unrestricted reuse permission.

The legal status of AI-generated output may vary by jurisdiction and tool terms, while SalimGPT's specific selection, editing, arrangement, and compiled production may involve separate rights.

27 What should I do if I want to use SalimGPT content in a commercial project?

A permission request should explain which content you want to use, how much, where it will appear, the intended purpose, whether the use is monetized, and whether the material will be modified.

Read the Content Use Policy
28 Can I reuse a third-party image that appears in a SalimGPT documentary?

SalimGPT may use a third-party asset under a specific license or permission, but that permission does not automatically transfer to the viewer.

The original rights holder and applicable license may need to be verified separately.
