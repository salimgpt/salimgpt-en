Profile

From research to publication— an integrated responsibility

Mohammad Salim is the founder and director of SalimGPT. He leads SalimGPT's creative, research, editorial, and production activities with the goal of producing research-based English documentaries.

He is directly involved in each stage of the long process that takes place before a documentary is published—topic selection, preliminary investigation, in-depth research, source verification, timeline development, scriptwriting, narration, visual planning, editing, and final review.

His objective is not simply to create information-heavy videos. The goal is to organize disconnected information into a clear narrative that gives viewers a deeper understanding of the subject.

THE IDEA

Why research-based documentary media

As information has exploded across digital platforms, misinformation, incomplete explanations, exaggerated claims, and presentations without context have also increased. Viewers may encounter several completely different accounts of the same event.

Under Mohammad Salim's direction, SalimGPT was developed as a platform where a subject is examined not only through viral information, but through its sources, history, timeline, evidence, and broader context.

“A documentary should not only explain what happened; it should also explain why it happened and the reality behind it.”
Beginning of the Journey

SalimGPT since December 2, 2025

On December 2, 2025, SalimGPT officially began its journey. With the release of its first research-based documentary on Gaza, the platform's editorial direction began to take shape.

Choosing a highly discussed subject surrounded by large amounts of conflicting information as the first documentary was important in defining SalimGPT's future research-oriented position.

Under Mohammad Salim's direction, SalimGPT later expanded its documentary scope into history, the historical rise of drugs, society, human civilization, technology, and other widely discussed social topics.

CREATIVE DIRECTION

Turning a subject into a story

An important part of Mohammad Salim's documentary direction is organizing the large amount of information found through research so that the subject remains coherent, relevant, and understandable to viewers.

He does not consider the sheer quantity of information to be the measure of a strong documentary. Determining which information is essential to the story, where further explanation is needed, and where visuals can make the subject clearer are also part of the production process.

The aim is to bring research, storytelling, and visual communication together within one structure.

Editorial Philosophy

Research first, conclusions later

01

Sources Matter

A claim should not be accepted as true simply because it exists. Sources, timing, and context should be checked whenever possible.

02

Information Needs Context

Understanding how an event began, when it changed, and what realities shaped it makes the information more accurate and meaningful.

03

The Viewer's Time Matters

After an engaging title, viewers should receive real information and something meaningful to learn. That is a goal of responsible publishing.

04

Correct When Necessary

If older information is shown to be incorrect by newer and stronger evidence, being prepared to correct it is important.

HUMAN-LED • AI-ASSISTED

Technology assists, human editorial judgment decides

AI-assisted tools may be used to organize research, analyze ideas, refine language, plan visuals, or support specific creative tasks.

However, decisions about which information is accepted, which statements are excluded, how the script is structured, and what form the final publication takes remain under Mohammad Salim's human editorial review.

WORKING PRINCIPLES

Five core working principles

01
Research First

02

Cross-check important information with multiple sources whenever possible.

03
