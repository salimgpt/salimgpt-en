DOCUMENTARY PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

From a single topic to a complete SalimGPT documentary

A SalimGPT documentary does not begin simply with scriptwriting or video editing. The topic is first understood, researched, and fact-checked before a narrative is built. Voice, visuals, and editing are then used to transform that research into a complete documentary.

Research → Verify → Write → Visualize → Publish

PRODUCTION PHILOSOPHY

Understand the subject first, then turn it into a story

Creating an engaging documentary requires more than attractive footage or fast editing. If the research is weak, the context is incomplete, or the narrative is unclear, strong visual quality alone cannot provide a solid foundation.

For this reason, SalimGPT places particular emphasis on research and verification before production. The research is then organized into a narrative that guides viewers through the subject step by step from beginning to end.

Research first. Storytelling second. Final publication comes after both.
10-STEP WORKFLOW

From idea to publication

A SalimGPT documentary generally moves through the following sequence. Some stages may become larger or smaller depending on the topic, but the importance of research and final review remains consistent.

01

Topic Selection

Determine whether a subject is suitable for research, important to viewers, and capable of supporting a deeper documentary explanation.

02

Preliminary Research

Understand the main history, major events, terminology, timeline, and potential source landscape surrounding the topic.

03

Deep Research

Gather detailed information from books, research papers, institutional documents, archives, reports, and other relevant sources.

04

Fact-Checking

Compare important claims, dates, historical events, and numerical information against multiple credible sources wherever possible.

05

Script Development

Turn verified and relevant information into SalimGPT's own wording, narrative structure, and explanatory flow.

06

Voice Production

Produce AI-generated or synthetic narration from the final script, adjusting pronunciation, pacing, and delivery where necessary.

07

Visual Planning

Plan which images, footage, maps, graphics, archives, or AI-generated visuals should accompany each part of the narration.

08

Editing & Assembly

Combine narration, visuals, graphics, sound, and documentary pacing into a complete video sequence.

09

Final Review

Recheck information, script, narration, visual context, pacing, possible errors, and overall presentation.

10

Publication

Prepare the final video, title, thumbnail, description, and necessary publication information before releasing it to viewers.

PHASE 01
TOPIC DISCOVERY

Topic selection forms the foundation of the documentary

At SalimGPT, a subject does not have to be selected merely because it is trending. When choosing a documentary topic, consideration is given to whether it has enough historical, scientific, social, or explanatory depth.

A strong topic often contains a larger history, transformation, controversy, or unfamiliar context behind something viewers may already recognize.

Preliminary research is then used to determine whether credible information is available and whether the evidence can support a coherent documentary narrative.

PHASE 03 • VERIFICATION

Not every piece of research carries the same evidentiary weight

Information found through an internet search is not automatically accepted as a final documentary fact. Source quality is considered, especially for important historical claims, dates, statistics, cause-and-effect relationships, and disputed information.

Wherever possible, an important claim is compared against another independent or more authoritative source. Attention is also given to the distinction between primary information, institutional sources, and secondary interpretation.

PHASE 04 • SCRIPT

Turning research material into a new documentary narrative

SalimGPT scripts are not created by copying paragraphs from sources or reproducing another documentary's script. After factual information is understood, a new narrative structure is developed.

The purpose of a script is not merely to list information. The narrative considers which question should open the story, when historical origins should appear, where curiosity should build, what context viewers need first, and what they should understand by the end.

Factual information is not the same as personal or editorial interpretation. Where analysis or opinion appears, it should not be used as a substitute for factual claims.

PRODUCTION STAGE

From script to a visual documentary

Once a script becomes production-ready after research and editorial review, voice and visual production begin.

Planning considers what type of image each scene needs, where AI-generated visuals may help, where explanatory graphics or archives should be used, and which parts of the narration require visual transitions.

The purpose is to ensure that visuals are not merely decorative, but actively help viewers understand the narration.

PHASE 05 • VOICE

AI-generated voice, human-directed narration

SalimGPT documentaries may use AI-generated or synthetic narration. However, the AI voice itself does not determine what the documentary says.

The final script, pronunciation, sentence pacing, pauses, emphasis, and overall tone are determined according to the documentary narrative.

In other words, voice is a production technology; the research, script, and editorial direction are established beforehand.

PHASE 06 • VISUAL PRODUCTION

Every visual should serve a narrative purpose

SalimGPT does not use visuals simply to fill the screen. Visual choices are considered in relation to what they help viewers understand from the narration.

AI-Generated Image

Historical, conceptual, or illustrative images may be generated where appropriate.

AI-Generated Footage

When direct footage is unavailable, visually relevant scenes may be generated to support the narration.

Archive & Reference

Archival, public-domain, or otherwise lawfully usable visual material may be used where appropriate.

Maps & Graphics

Maps, diagrams, and explanatory graphics may be used to clarify locations, timelines, comparisons, or complex ideas.

Visual Modification

Cropping, framing, movement, timing, or composition may be adjusted to fit the documentary's visual language.

Context Check

Illustrative visuals are reviewed so they do not unnecessarily mislead viewers into treating them as factual archival evidence.

PHASE 07 • EDITING

Bringing every element into one narrative rhythm

During editing, voice and visuals are not simply placed side by side. Narration rhythm, visual changes, information density, and viewer attention are considered together.

Editing decisions include where scenes should remain longer, where faster transitions are needed, where maps or text support information, and where visual silence may be more effective.

The goal is to prevent the documentary from becoming unnecessarily slow, while also avoiding such rapid pacing that viewers cannot process the information.

VIEWER RETENTION

Keeping viewers engaged without sacrificing information

SalimGPT documentaries are structured to move viewer curiosity forward step by step.

However, viewer retention should not depend on removing factual context, relying only on sensational claims, or arranging scenes in a misleading way.

Instead, engagement is built through hooks, questions, visual rhythm, historical progression, and narrative payoff across the full video.

CONTEXT + CURIOSITY + VISUAL + STORY = ENGAGEMENT

HUMAN + AI
HUMAN-LED • AI-ASSISTED

AI is part of the workflow, not the director

AI may assist SalimGPT production with research support, language support, image generation, illustrative footage, synthetic narration, or other technical tasks.

However, final editorial control remains human when deciding which topic becomes a documentary, which sources are acceptable, which information enters the script, what interpretation is presented, which visuals are used, and whether the final video should be published.

ONE COMPLETE PROCESS

A documentary is never the result of only one task

Every stage, from topic selection to publication, affects what follows. Weak research can create a weak script; a weak script can make visuals directionless; and a weak final review can expose even strong production to factual errors.

For this reason, SalimGPT treats the entire process as a connected system where research, verification, storytelling, technology, and human editorial judgment work together.
