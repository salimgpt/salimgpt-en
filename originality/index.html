ORIGINALITY & CREATIVE IDENTITY

Not copying, not imitation— SalimGPT's own production language

SalimGPT's principle is not to directly copy another creator's video, audio, script, editing style, or complete presentation. Each documentary is developed through independent research, an original narrative structure, a newly written script, AI-assisted visual production, synthetic narration, and an independent editing language.

OUR ORIGINALITY PRINCIPLE

Not repeating someone else's work— interpreting information in a new way

Many creators around the world may work on the same history, scientific information, or event. Sharing the same subject matter does not mean the presentation must also be the same. SalimGPT does not follow that approach.

After gathering information on a topic, SalimGPT independently decides which context should come first, which information requires explanation, which events should be connected within the same narrative, and which kinds of visuals can communicate the subject most effectively.

Information may be universal; research, interpretation, language, narrative, and presentation can still be original.
OUR PRINCIPLE

What SalimGPT does not directly copy or imitate

Originality is not simply a new thumbnail or a new voice. The core principle is that an entire documentary production should not become a replica of someone else's work.

Video

Republishing another creator's complete video or sequence of scenes as a new SalimGPT video is not part of the SalimGPT production model.

Audio

Another person's narration or voice recording is not collected and directly reused as SalimGPT documentary narration.

Script

An article, documentary script, or another creator's writing is not copied word for word and published as a SalimGPT script.

Narrative Structure

Instead of imitating another documentary's chapter sequence, storytelling flow, or scene-by-scene structure, an independent structure is developed.

Editing Style

Rather than recreating another channel's transitions, pacing, visual language, and complete editing identity, SalimGPT maintains its own rhythm.

Creative Identity

Another documentary brand's visual identity, presentation formula, or complete creative character is not directly adopted.

SOURCES ≠ SCRIPT

Sources provide information; the script is created independently

Research-based documentary production necessarily relies on multiple sources. Factual information may be gathered from books, research papers, government documents, historical archives, news reports, institutional information, and other relevant sources.

However, the language of a source is not automatically treated as SalimGPT's final script. After understanding and comparing the collected information and determining the necessary context, the script is developed in new language, with a new narrative flow, and with an independent interpretation.

Where analysis or explanation is required, SalimGPT attempts to maintain a distinction between factual information and editorial interpretation. Personal or editorial perspectives, when present, are treated as part of the explanation rather than a substitute for evidence.

AI-ASSISTED VISUAL PRODUCTION

Visuals created, selected, and modified according to production needs

AI visual tools may play an important supporting role in SalimGPT documentary production. When suitable direct footage of a historical scene, conceptual event, past environment, or explanatory situation is unavailable, a visually meaningful representation may be created.

Images or footage generated with AI are not always placed directly into the final video. Depending on production needs, crop, composition, movement, timing, visual treatment, contextual placement, and editing may be used to integrate them into the documentary's visual language.

These visuals are not intended to mislead viewers into believing they are authentic archival footage. Where appropriate, their purpose is to provide illustrative representations that help explain an idea, environment, or event.

SYNTHETIC NARRATION

The voice may be AI-generated, but narration direction belongs to SalimGPT

SalimGPT documentary narration may use AI-generated or synthetic voice technology. However, voice technology is only one production tool used for narration.

What will be said, which words will be used, how the script will flow, where pauses are needed, where emotional emphasis belongs, and what tone the documentary should maintain are determined by the script and editorial direction.

In other words, synthetic voice may provide the voice of the final documentary, but it does not determine the documentary's claims, research, or editorial judgement.

FOOTAGE & VISUAL MATERIAL

Transformation, context, and necessity matter when visual material is used

Documentary production may sometimes require archival material, historical imagery, licensed material, public-domain resources, or third-party visual material that can lawfully be used.

When such material is used, SalimGPT's policy is not to take long portions from another person's video and republish them unchanged.

Where appropriate, visual material is connected to documentary narration, analysis, commentary, or explanatory context and may be integrated through editing, framing, timing, composition, or other creative treatment as part of a new documentary context.

How any material may be used depends on its source, license, applicable rights, and the context of use.

Contextual Use

ILLUSTRATIVE REPRESENTATION

When real footage does not exist, explanatory scenes may be used

Many historical events, ways of life from centuries ago, chemical processes, conceptual scenes, and other subjects were never captured in direct video footage.

In such cases, SalimGPT may use AI-generated illustrative scenes, conceptual reconstructions, or visually meaningful representations to help viewers understand the narration.

These visuals are part of the documentary's explanatory language and should not automatically be interpreted as authentic historical camera footage.

HUMAN EDITORIAL CONTROL

AI may generate, but final decisions remain human

Even when AI is used to generate visuals or narration, SalimGPT documentaries are not produced through a fully automated AI-controlled publishing process.

Topic selection, deciding which information is acceptable, what should be excluded, script language, personal or editorial interpretation, visual selection, scene placement, editing, and final publication remain human creative and editorial decisions.

SALIMGPT ORIGINALITY

The goal of a SalimGPT documentary is to stand on its own identity

SalimGPT does not operate on a model of republishing another creator's work under its own name. Research is conducted, sources are analyzed, an original script is written, visuals are created or selected, synthetic narration may be used, and the complete documentary is independently edited and structured.

Technology may change, AI tools may become more advanced, and methods of visual production may evolve over time. But SalimGPT's core principle remains— independent research, original interpretation, and independent presentation.

TRANSPARENCY

Originality does not mean denying sources

Factual sources used in research, lawfully usable third-party material, public-domain resources, or licensed material may be used where appropriate. SalimGPT's originality does not mean denying the existence of those sources. It means avoiding the direct copying of another creator's complete creative work and independently using information and lawful material within a new documentary context.
