IMPERSONATION

If you see a fake account, verify it through an official link

A page or account does not automatically become official simply because it uses SalimGPT's logo, name, or visual identity.

If such an account misleadingly presents itself as an official SalimGPT representative, the relevant platform's reporting tools may be used.

If you are unsure, you may send the account link through SalimGPT's Contact page and request clarification.

CONTENT CONTEXT

A short social post is not a substitute for a full documentary

Because of character limits, format, duration, and audience behavior, a social-media post may not always contain the complete context of a documentary.

When a complex topic appears in a short clip or condensed post, it is important to review the full documentary, related written page, or original source context.

SalimGPT's deeper research-based presentation may be available on the website and in full documentary format.

COMMENTS & COMMUNITY

Audience discussion is welcome, but comments are not automatically SalimGPT's own statements

Comments, replies, or community contributions on social platforms are generally the statements of the users who posted them.

Third-party comments or audience opinions should not be treated as SalimGPT's official editorial position.

Spam, impersonation, harassment, illegal material, or platform-rule violations may be moderated according to the relevant platform's policies.
