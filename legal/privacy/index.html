PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy matters— so it should be clear what data may exist, why it may be processed, and where control is limited

This Privacy Policy explains what types of technical information may be generated when you use the SalimGPT website, how information you voluntarily provide may be handled, what role third-party platforms play, and what you should know about privacy.

PRIVACY AT A GLANCE

In brief

SalimGPT is a documentary media website. The current static website experience does not require users to create an account, log in, provide a password, or submit payment information.

However, website hosting, browser communication, linked social platforms, or services added in the future may process their own technical data.

SalimGPT's approach: do not request more personal data than necessary, do not request sensitive credentials, and do not present a third-party service's privacy responsibilities as SalimGPT's own.
