CONTENT USE POLICY

Viewing and sharing are easy— reuse also has rules

SalimGPT documentaries, written articles, script expressions, research-based presentations, thumbnails, graphics, logos, AI-assisted images, generated footage, website content, and other original production materials may be publicly accessible, but that does not automatically make them open for unrestricted copying, reuploading, republication, or commercial reuse. This page explains which uses are generally acceptable, when attribution is needed, when permission should be requested, and which uses are not permitted.

CORE PRINCIPLE

Reference and sharing are encouraged— copying and impersonation are not

SalimGPT generally supports responsible sharing that helps people discover content, discuss it, use it as a reference, mention it in educational contexts, and direct others back to the original publication.

Republishing an entire documentary, article, script, thumbnail, generated visual, or other original work on your own channel, page, website, or commercial product is a different matter.

Core principle: send traffic to the source, credit the original creator, and request permission before substantial reuse.

ATTRIBUTION

Credit is not only a name— it should also lead back to the source

When using a limited quotation, excerpt, screenshot, or part of a research-based presentation from SalimGPT, you should, where possible, mention the name “SalimGPT,” the relevant documentary or article title, and the original source link.

Writing only “Source: Internet” or “Source: Google” is not sufficient attribution, because a search engine is not itself the original creator or rights holder.

Attribution should make it easy for the audience to understand where the original publication can be found.

ATTRIBUTION FORMAT

A simple structure for giving credit

SIMPLE FORMAT SalimGPT — [Documentary Title]

The exact format does not need to be identical in every situation. Presentation may vary depending on the platform, publication style, or academic citation format.

However, the credit should not be hidden, misleading, or presented in a way that makes the user appear to be the original creator.

QUOTATION

Short quotes may be used— not the entire script

A limited quotation for review, criticism, research discussion, classroom use, reporting, or reference is not the same as full republication.

A quotation should be limited to what is reasonably necessary to explain the relevant context and should not become a substitute for the original article, script, or documentary.

Long transcripts, full scripts, chapter-by-chapter copying, or extensive quotation that removes the need to view the original publication may require permission.

VIDEO & DOCUMENTARY

Linking to a documentary is not the same as reuploading its file

Sharing the official SalimGPT YouTube or other platform link is generally acceptable. Using an official embed feature to display the video on a website may also be allowed under the platform's rules.

Downloading a video and uploading it in full to another YouTube channel, Facebook page, TikTok account, website, app, or other distribution channel is not ordinary sharing.

Full reuploads, mirror uploads, compilation reuploads, or edits where a substantial part of a SalimGPT documentary becomes the core content of another publication may require permission.

USE CONTEXT

The same content may be treated differently depending on how it is used

Using a documentary screenshot as a reference in a review article is not the same as using that screenshot as the main artwork in a commercial advertisement.

Likewise, using a short quotation in academic discussion is not the same as copying an entire script and publishing it as your own narration.

The amount, purpose, presentation, audience, and commercial nature of the use therefore matter.

SCREENSHOTS

Screenshots are also a form of content reuse

Screenshots of the SalimGPT website, documentaries, infographics, or articles may be used in a limited way for review, commentary, reporting, or reference.

Screenshots should not be cropped or edited in a way that changes the original meaning, creates a misleading statement, or implies SalimGPT endorsement.

Commercial artwork, advertisements, product packaging, or extensive screenshot republication may require separate permission.

THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL

Not every asset inside a SalimGPT documentary belongs to SalimGPT

A documentary may contain licensed archival footage, permission-based photographs, public-domain material, open-licensed graphics, or other lawfully usable third-party assets.

SalimGPT may be permitted to use such assets under a specific license or permission, but website visitors or viewers do not automatically receive the same reuse rights.

Before extracting a third-party photograph or footage from a SalimGPT publication for another use, the original rights holder and applicable license should be checked separately.

EDUCATIONAL USE

Reference in education and research is generally welcome

Teachers, students, researchers, and learners may use SalimGPT documentaries or articles as references in discussions, assignments, presentations, or research bibliographies.

Providing the original link, title, and SalimGPT attribution is good practice.

However, an educational purpose does not automatically permit downloading full documentaries, republishing complete scripts, or redistributing restricted third-party assets without limitation.

BRAND USE

The SalimGPT name and logo may be referenced— but not used for impersonation

The SalimGPT brand name may be mentioned in articles, reviews, citations, or commentary about SalimGPT.

However, the SalimGPT logo, wordmark, or visual identity should not be used to create websites, channels, products, or accounts that could be mistaken for official SalimGPT publications.

The “GPT” in SalimGPT does not indicate official endorsement, ownership, or partnership with OpenAI or ChatGPT.

TRANSLATION

Translation may also be derivative reuse

Translating an entire SalimGPT article, script, or documentary transcript into another language and publishing it as your own publication may be considered substantial reuse.

Translating a short quotation with source attribution is not the same type of use as translating and republishing a complete documentary script.

Permission should be requested for full translations, dubbed versions, subtitle redistribution, or localized republication.

PERMISSION ≠ OWNERSHIP

Receiving permission does not transfer ownership

If SalimGPT grants a user permission to use a particular image, clip, article excerpt, or other content, that permission may be limited to the specified use.

It does not automatically transfer SalimGPT's underlying ownership, attribution requirements, or other rights to the user unless a separate written agreement clearly states otherwise.

NO IMPLIED LICENSE

Being visible on the website does not itself create a license

The fact that SalimGPT content is publicly accessible on a website, YouTube, or social platform does not itself grant permission for broad redistribution, resale, sublicensing, or commercial republication.

Even if content is technically downloadable, the ability to download it does not determine ownership or reuse rights.

If there is uncertainty, requesting permission before substantial reuse is the clearest approach.
