Who We Are

Why SalimGPT

Information is now within everyone's reach. But as the volume of information has increased, so have misinformation, rumors, claims without context, exaggerated headlines, and deliberate attempts to mislead people.

An event can reach millions of people through social media within minutes. Yet the history, evidence, timeline, causes, and real context behind that event often remain outside the discussion. In many cases, a partial truth is presented as the complete truth.

This is where SalimGPT defines its responsibility. Our goal is not simply to tell an engaging story, but to understand a subject as thoroughly as possible through research, fact-checking, and relevant sources, and then present it clearly to the audience.

SalimGPT documentaries are therefore primarily for people who do not only want to know “what happened”; they also want to understand “why it happened, where it began, how it changed, and what impact it created”.

Our Journey

December 2, 2025 — The Official Launch of SalimGPT

On December 2, 2025, SalimGPT officially began its documentary journey. On that day, with the release of its first research-based documentary focused on Gaza, SalimGPT appeared before audiences with an independent documentary media identity.

Gaza was a subject where history, geopolitics, humanitarian crisis, international responses, and a vast amount of conflicting information were deeply interconnected. As a result, SalimGPT's core philosophy became clear from its very first publication—not to understand a widely discussed issue only through emotion or conventional narratives, but to examine it through its background and broader context.

That beginning later led SalimGPT toward deeper documentary work on history, society, drugs, human civilization, technology, and various widely discussed social realities.

RESEARCH FIRST

Research Is the Foundation of Every Documentary

A SalimGPT documentary does not begin at the video editing table—it begins at the research table.

After a topic is selected, its basic history, timeline, major events, and relevant claims are first identified. Depending on the subject, books, research papers, government or institutional documents, reports from international organizations, historical archives, news reports, and relevant web sources may then be reviewed.

SalimGPT does not automatically treat a statement found in a single source as the final truth. Important claims are cross-checked with multiple sources whenever possible.

We treat information collection and fact-checking as two different tasks, because simply finding information online does not mean that information is accurate.

FROM RESEARCH TO STORY

Not Just a Collection of Facts—A Complete Story

Hundreds of facts discovered during research do not automatically become a documentary. Building connections among those facts, separating essential information from unnecessary details, and creating a clear narrative structure are important stages of documentary production.

SalimGPT generally organizes a topic through its origin, historical development, major changes, turning points, present reality, and impact.

After research, information is not copied directly. It is transformed into an original script using a new structure and language. Voice, visuals, graphics, and editing are then used to turn it into a complete documentary experience.

VISUAL STORYTELLING

Visuals Are Not Used Only for Aesthetics

At SalimGPT, images, video, maps, graphics, archival materials, or AI-assisted visuals are primarily used to make information clearer and help audiences understand the story.

A visual that does not move the story forward or help explain information does not need to be used simply because it looks good.

HUMAN-LED • AI-ASSISTED

Technology Assists, Humans Decide

In modern documentary production, AI may be used as an assistive technology for organizing research, analyzing ideas, refining language, planning visuals, and supporting specific creative tasks.

However, AI-generated output is not itself the editor of SalimGPT. Human editorial judgment determines which information is accepted, how the script is written, which claims are publishable, and what the final documentary becomes.

Editorial Position

Letting the Evidence Lead the Information

For SalimGPT, neutrality does not mean forcing every claim to receive equal weight. It means considering the available evidence, reliable sources, and context in order to present a subject as accurately as possible.

A claim does not become true simply because it is widely circulated. Likewise, when different interpretations exist, those differences may be presented with appropriate context when necessary.

OUR COMMITMENT

Audience Trust Is the Most Valuable Asset

SalimGPT does not claim to be free from error. New information may emerge in research and history, existing information may be corrected, and new interpretations of events may become available.

Our commitment is not to intentionally create false information, not to publish important claims without verification, to maintain the distinction between fact and opinion, and to remain prepared to correct significant errors when identified.

In the long term, the greatest strength of a media platform is not views—it is trust.
