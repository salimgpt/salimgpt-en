VISUAL & MEDIA SOURCES

Where a visual comes from is also part of a documentary

The visuals used in SalimGPT documentaries do not come from a single source. Visual production is completed through a combination of valid and creative methods including topic research, Google and other search/discovery platforms, specific websites, archives, reference images, permission-based third-party media, licensed materials, AI-generated images and prompt-driven AI footage.

CORE PRINCIPLE

Finding something on Google does not mean having permission to use it

SalimGPT does not consider any image or footage automatically free to use simply because it was found through Google Search, Google Images or any other search engine. Search engines are tools for discovery and reference finding; they do not provide copyright ownership or usage permission for media.

Therefore, when a specific image or footage needs to be directly used in a documentary, efforts are made to understand the original source, creator, photographer, publisher, archive, agency or rights holder as clearly as possible.

Where permission or licensing is required, the usage follows the conditions defined by the source.

Discovery ≠ Permission. Reference ≠ Ownership. Rights and conditions matter before use.

PHASE 01
DISCOVERY & REFERENCE

First, we discover what the visual should look like

While planning visuals for a specific section of a documentary script, references may be collected from Google, Google Images, search engines, archives, institutional websites, news archives, museum collections, historical materials and topic-specific websites.

The main purpose of this stage is not always to directly download an image. In many cases, references help understand how clothing looked in a certain period, how architecture appeared, what a historical environment looked like, the visual character of a location, the structure of an object, or the possible mood of an event.

This visual information can later support prompt design, scene planning, AI generation or graphic construction as a reference.

SEARCH ENGINE USE

Google is a discovery tool, not a media license

SalimGPT may use Google Search or Google Images during topic research or visual research. However, an image appearing on Google does not mean that it is owned by SalimGPT or freely reusable.

Before direct usage, important steps include reaching the original media source, understanding source terms and obtaining permission when necessary.

DIRECT MEDIA USE

Sometimes original images or footage are directly used

Some historical photographs, rare images, archive materials or specific footage may be more suitable to use from the original source rather than recreating them with AI. In such cases, using the original source can also be important for factual accuracy.

In these situations, SalimGPT reviews the usage conditions of the source. When permission from a rights holder is required, permission is obtained and the media is used according to the applicable permission or license terms.

Some sources require attribution, some allow usage only in specific contexts, some provide commercial licenses, and in certain cases payment may be required to obtain usage rights.

PHASE 02
PROMPT DESIGN

After studying references, instructions for new scenes are created

An important part of SalimGPT's AI-generated visual production is prompt design. Using only a general instruction like "create a historical scene" may not produce precise visuals suitable for a documentary.

Therefore, according to narration and research context, details such as the time period, environment, subject, clothing, architecture, camera angle, lens feel, lighting, composition, movement, atmosphere and visual realism are specified as needed.

Reference images or research visuals may provide factual hints about a scene; however, the final AI output is created according to a new production direction.

IMAGE → VIDEO

Creating cinematic footage from static visuals

In some cases, an image is first created or legally obtained for documentary production. That image can then be used as a base visual to create a separate motion prompt for an image-to-video capable AI system.

The motion prompt may define elements such as camera movement, subject motion, environmental changes, depth, atmosphere, speed and scene stability.

Using an appropriate AI model or generation system, multiple variations may be created. The most suitable output is selected based on the documentary narrative and further generation, trimming or editing is performed when necessary.

GENERATED MEDIA

AI output is not the final footage

The first footage generated by an AI system is not always directly used in the documentary timeline.

Generated footage is reviewed for visual anatomy, historical details, unwanted objects, inconsistencies, motion errors, lighting and contextual alignment with the narration.

If required, prompts are modified and generation is repeated, or editing is used to adjust crop, speed, framing, duration, transitions and composition.

AI footage created through its own production process becomes part of SalimGPT's media library

AI images and footage created for SalimGPT through its own research, scene planning, prompt design, generation direction, selection and editing process are stored and used as SalimGPT production assets.

However, the legal copyright or ownership status of generative AI outputs may vary depending on country, jurisdiction, applicable law and the terms of the AI service used. Therefore, where applicable, SalimGPT uses generated assets according to the relevant tool's usage and commercial terms.

PAID RIGHTS & LICENSING

Paying for quality visuals can also be part of professional production

Not all usable media is available for free. If a rare archive, licensed photograph, professional footage or commercial-use asset is important for a documentary, payment may be made to obtain valid usage rights.

Paying for media does not automatically mean unlimited ownership. The license terms define what is allowed, for how long, on which platforms and what type of modifications are permitted.

Therefore, SalimGPT considers license conditions an essential part of production even when paid media is used.

NOT A RE-UPLOAD MODEL

Taking another creator's video and publishing it with a new title is not SalimGPT's production model

SalimGPT documentary production does not depend on taking complete videos, sequences or finished documentaries from other creators and re-uploading them under its own name.

Even when third-party materials are used, they are incorporated as specific elements within a larger documentary structure containing research, narration, explanation and editorial organization. Applicable permission, licensing and source conditions are considered.

ILLUSTRATIVE AI VISUAL

When real footage is unavailable, explanatory scenes may be used

Many historical events, ancient societies, private environments or situations do not have direct camera footage. In such cases, AI-generated illustrative or conceptual scenes may be used to explain documentary narratives.

The purpose of these visuals is to help viewers understand time, environment, events or concepts — not to create fabricated archival evidence.

When context requires it, AI-generated reconstruction is not presented in a misleading way as actual historical camera footage. This editorial principle is followed to maintain clarity.

AI systems can generate scenes, but decisions about whether a visual accurately represents the documentary message, whether an output is misleading, which scenes should be removed and which footage should appear in the final timeline are part of human editorial review.

SalimGPT's overall visual direction, prompt development, output selection, modification and final documentary integration remain under a human-led production process.

TRANSPARENCY

Originality does not mean denying sources

SalimGPT creates its own documentary productions, but documentary creation naturally requires existing knowledge, archives, historical materials, references, licensed media and third-party sources.

Therefore, SalimGPT does not claim that every visual element used in a documentary is always originally created by SalimGPT.

The distinction is that research, scene planning, prompt design, AI generation, production selection, editing and final presentation are created under SalimGPT's own creative direction. When third-party media is included, applicable source rights and conditions are considered separately.
